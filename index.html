Questions

Frequently asked

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are delivered within minutes after the transaction is confirmed on the network (up to ~10 minutes on busy chains).

Which payment networks do you accept?

We accept USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20). In your wallet, make sure the selected network is Ethereum (ERC-20) — sending on any other network may result in lost funds.

Can I get a refund?

Digital goods are non-refundable once delivered. If a file is broken or doesn't match the description, contact us and we'll fix or replace it.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up required. Just order, pay, and receive your download link by email.

Can I use the products commercially?

Yes — every product includes a commercial license for unlimited personal and client projects.
