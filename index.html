Questions

Frequently asked

How fast is delivery?

Most orders are delivered within minutes after the transaction confirms on the network (up to ~10 minutes when Ethereum is busy).

Which payment networks do you accept?

USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20). In your wallet, make sure the selected network is Ethereum (ERC-20) — sending on another network may lose your funds.

Can I use a theme on multiple sites?

Yes. The GPL license lets you use each theme on unlimited sites that you own. Reselling or redistributing the files is not allowed.

Do I need any plugins?

Only WooCommerce for shop themes, and it's free. Everything else works out of the box — no paid plugin required.

What if a theme doesn't work for me?

Digital goods are non-refundable once delivered, but if a file is broken or doesn't match its description we'll fix or replace it free of charge.
