Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026 — plain English, no tracking tricks.

1. What we collect

We only receive the information you voluntarily provide when ordering: your email address and your cryptocurrency transaction ID. We do not require an account and we do not collect names, phone numbers or postal addresses.

2. How we use it

  • To verify your payment and deliver your download link.
  • To answer support requests you send us.
  • To keep basic order records for accounting and fraud prevention.

3. Payments

Payments are made in USDT on the Ethereum network. Blockchain transactions are public by nature; we only read the transaction ID you submit. We never see or store your wallet's private keys.

4. Cookies & analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies and does not use invasive tracking. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated before they go live.

5. Third parties

Your email is handled by your own email provider when you contact us. Files are delivered by direct download link. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

6. Data retention

Order records are kept as long as needed for support and legal obligations, then deleted.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@novabits.store.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to support@novabits.store.
